## Full description (up to 4000 characters)

```
Blocks — Block Puzzle is a classic 8x8 block game you can play offline, with no wifi needed.

Drag blocks from the tray onto the board, fill complete rows and columns to clear them and score points. The game ends when none of the three blocks fits. It sounds simple — and it is almost impossible to put down.

WHY PEOPLE KEEP PLAYING

• Easy to learn: no tutorial needed, you get it in 30 seconds.
• Play offline: in the subway, on a plane or wherever there is no Wi-Fi.
• No time pressure: think as long as you like, it is your puzzle.
• Combo multipliers: clear several lines with one move for a big score boost.
• 7 board themes: from classic to neon, ocean, forest and galaxy.
• Stats and 8 achievements: best score, lines, combos and play time.
• A daily bonus for coming back.
• Progress is saved: your records and coins stay after you close the app.

HOW TO PLAY

1. Drag a block from the tray to empty cells on the 8x8 board.
2. A completed row or column disappears and gives you points.
3. Clear multiple lines in one move for a combo multiplier.
4. When no block fits, the game is over. Watch a short video to continue playing or start a new game with doubled coins.

Spend the coins you earn on board themes: Neon, Candy, Ocean, Forest, Mono and Galaxy.

WHO IS IT FOR

For everyone who enjoys block puzzles, tile games, brain teasers and sudoku: a calm logic game with no rush and no complicated rules. Great as a five-minute game before bed or on the road.
```
