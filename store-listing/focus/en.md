## Full description

Take a short mental break with Focus. Choose one of three simple exercises: remember a sequence of glowing tiles, spot one symbol among similar ones, or pick the ink color instead of reading the word.

Each activity has short rounds, clear instructions, three attempts and an easy-to-read result. Visual search gradually becomes more demanding. The daily challenge changes by date and is shared by everyone on the same calendar day, so you can return for a brief practice and keep track of your personal streak.

**Inside the app:**
• three self-contained activities: memory, visual search and color attention;
• a daily challenge and day streak;
• score, local statistics and personal best;
• separate sound and haptic controls;
• large touch targets and layouts for small screens;
• offline gameplay — an internet connection is only needed to load ads.

These activities are for entertainment and mental warm-ups. Focus is not a medical tool, does not measure intelligence, and makes no diagnostic or therapeutic claims.

Progress is stored on the device. No account, contacts, precise location or user-generated posts are required. Ads are provided by Yandex; you can choose whether to allow personalized advertising and change that choice in settings.
